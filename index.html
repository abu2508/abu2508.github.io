About

Systems thinking, applied to AI.

I'm an AI/ML Engineer at Tata Consultancy Services, working on production GenAI platforms for enterprise environments. My work spans RAG systems, LLM orchestration, cloud-native architecture, AI evaluation and workflow automation.

My path into AI started outside traditional software engineering. With a B.E. in Civil Engineering and a design patent for a Smart Solar Bench, I developed a systems-oriented approach to complex problems. Working directly with enterprise IT workflows and incidents then led me toward AI-driven automation.

“I approach AI architecture the way I approach structural design: understand the system, identify the load, and make every component work together.”

Deterministic where possible

Hard rules and gates for what must be right; LLM reasoning only where it adds value.

Security at the data layer

Tenant isolation enforced in the vector store, not left to prompt instructions.

Verifiable by design

Citations, conflict detection and evidence checks so answers can be audited.
